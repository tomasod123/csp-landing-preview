Privacy notice for this website

Last updated 8 October 2026

This notice covers the Clinic Success Partners pages on clinicsuccesspartners.com, uk.clinicsuccesspartners.com and apply.clinicsuccesspartners.com, including the application form, the booking calendar and the pages you see after you book. It is written in plain English. The organisation responsible for your data (the "controller") is Meadu Digital Limited, an Irish company (CRO 727990), registered office Lakeview, Claregalway, Co. Galway, H91 P8NT, Ireland, trading as Clinic Success Partners.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, email, phone number, clinic name and website, and your answers to the application questions.
  • What your browser gives us: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, how you arrived (for example an ad or a link), and cookie or pixel identifiers where you have agreed to them.
  • If you book a call: the time you pick, and notes and recordings from the call where we tell you at the start that it is recorded.

Do you have to give us this data?

No. But without your name, email and phone number we cannot book or run your call.

Why we use it, and what we rely on

  • To check whether your clinic is a fit, prepare your Aesthetic Clinic Overhaul and run the call. Legal basis: taking steps you asked for before any contract.
  • To reply to your enquiry and keep a record of it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running our business. You can object at any time (see below).
  • To send you marketing by email, text or WhatsApp, and to run advertising and analytics cookies. Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.

Messages from us

We will email, text or WhatsApp you about your application and your call. Those messages are needed to run the call you asked for. We only send marketing by email, text or WhatsApp if you have agreed to it. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text, or tell us on WhatsApp, and they stop. Click "unsubscribe" in any email. You can also email tomas@clinicsuccesspartners.com. Saying no to marketing does not stop you booking a call.

Your right to object

You can object to us using your data for our own interests, including any marketing, at any time. We will stop unless we have a strong legal reason not to. Email tomas@clinicsuccesspartners.com.

Who sees it

Our team, and the service providers who run our systems for us: HighLevel Inc. (our CRM, forms, calendars and messaging, United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses), Meta Platforms Ireland (advertising measurement, only if you consent to the pixel), Google (analytics and calendar), Vidalytics (video hosting), and our email, calling and note-taking tools. They act on our instructions. We do not sell your data. We do not share your clinic details with other clinics.

Where it goes

Some providers are in the United States. Where data leaves the EU or UK we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, its UK extension, or standard contractual clauses.

Call recordings and AI note-takers

We only record a call if we tell you at the start. An AI note-taking tool may join to write a summary for us. It works only for us and does not use your data to train its own models. We keep recordings and notes for up to 24 months after our last contact, then delete them unless you become a client.

How long we keep it

Application and call data: up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted unless you become a client. Advertising measurement data: as set by the platform, typically up to 13 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, fix it, delete it, limit how we use it, move it to another service, and stop using it for marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time. That does not affect what we did before you withdrew. Email tomas@clinicsuccesspartners.com and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or, if you are in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies and pixels

We use a Meta pixel and Google Analytics to see which ads and pages bring enquiries. We switch them on only where you have agreed through the cookie choice on our pages. If a page shows no cookie choice, these tools are switched off on that page. Essential cookies that make the pages, form and booking calendar work are always on. You can block cookies in your browser at any time and still use every page. Meta's own policy explains what it does with pixel data.

Age

Our pages are for people who run or work in a clinic. They are not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

We keep your data in trusted systems with passwords and limits on who can see it. No system on the internet is 100% safe. If your data is ever put at risk, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Who to contact

For privacy questions, write to Meadu Digital Limited, an Irish company (CRO 727990), registered office Lakeview, Claregalway, Co. Galway, H91 P8NT, Ireland, or email tomas@clinicsuccesspartners.com.

Changes
